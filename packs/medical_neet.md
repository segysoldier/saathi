## Jaanchne layak facts

MCQ ke saare jawaab standard NCERT facts hain aur inme koi shak nahi. Story ki kuch baatein founder ek baar dekh lein:
1. Din 2: "O group universal donor" NCERT ki simple line hai. Asal mein clinically O-negative ko universal donor kehte hain, aur emergency mein bhi cross-match hota hai. Story mein yeh sahi dikhaya hai, par MCQ simple NCERT version follow karta hai.
