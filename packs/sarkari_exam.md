3. **Running joke:** Mummy ka tiffin, jisme har din pichhle din se zyada parathe hote hain ("office walon ke liye bhi"). Saath me hain: Sharma ji (head clerk, 28 saal ki service, laal register), Munni didi (office ki sab-jaanne-wali), aur Collector Sahab (Badri kaka ki bakri).

### Din 1: Pehla din, pehli bakri

**THAKA pack (sirf kahani):**
Subah 5 baje Ratanganj se bus pakdi. Mummy ne tiffin me 14 parathe pack kiye the. "Office walon ke liye bhi hain, akele mat khaana."

Chaar ghante baad Bhaironpur Block office. Gate pe ek safed bakri baithi thi, gale me ghanti. Ankit itna nervous tha ki uske munh se nikla, "Namaste ji sir."

Bakri ne dekha. Boli, "Meh."

Peeche se hasi aayi. Munni didi, haath me jhaadu. "Yeh Collector Sahab hain. Badri kaka ki bakri. Roz subah inspection pe aati hain."

Andar head clerk Sharma ji baithe the. Chashma naak ki nok pe, saamne ek mota laal register. Ankit ne kaha, "Ji sir, main Ankit, naya ADO."

Sharma ji ne upar dekha. "Sir mat bolo, officer tum ho. Aur jis kursi pe baith rahe ho, woh meri hai."

Ankit kone wali kursi pe gaya. Uski ek taang chhoti thi.

Lunch me 14 parathe chhe minute me khatam. Sharma ji ne bhi do khaaye, bina kuch bole.

Shaam ko jaate waqt Sharma ji ne ek dhool bhari file uski table pe rakhi. "Raat ko padh lena. Isme ek naam hai jise teen saal se koi dhoondh nahi paaya."

Aaj ka rest poora. Kal milte hain.

**THEEK add-on (ek mazedaar sawal):**
Agar tumhare office ke gate pe roz ek bakri "inspection" karne aaye, to tum uska kya naam rakhte?
*Reveal:* Bhaironpur walon ne "Collector Sahab" isliye rakha kyunki woh bina bataye aati hai, har kamre me jhaankti hai, aur kisi ki nahi sunti.
