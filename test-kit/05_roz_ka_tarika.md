# Roz ka tarika (test ke Din 3 se 12)

Aapka samay: lagbhag 1 se 1.5 ghanta roz.

## Subah (10 minute)

1. Aaj kaun sa "Din N" hai, dekho. Teeno pack file kholo (`packs/` folder).
2. Aaj ka episode ek baar khud padho. Koi galti dikhe to theek karo.
3. Jo log voice note chahte hain, unke liye episode record kar lo (2-3 minute, apni awaaz me, aaram se).

## Har insaan ke khatarnak waqt par

Unke bataye waqt se 5 minute pehle bhejo. Teen message, isi kram me:

**Message 1: haal puchho**

> Saathi yahan. Abhi kaisa haal hai?
> 1 = thaka hoon
> 2 = theek hoon
> 3 = taaza hoon

**Message 2: jawab ke hisaab se pack**

| Jawab | Kya bhejna hai |
|---|---|
| 1 (thaka) | Sirf THAKA pack (kahani). Aur kuch nahi |
| 2 (theek) | THAKA pack, phir THEEK add-on ka sawal. Jawab aaye to reveal bhejo |
| 3 (taaza) | THAKA pack chhota karke (ya poora), phir TAAZA add-on. Jawab aaye to sahi/galat aur ek line ki wajah |
| Koi jawab nahi (10 min) | THAKA pack bhej do, bina kuch kahe |

**Message 3: pack ke baad ek sawal**

> Halka laga? (haan / nahi)

Hafte me 3 baar (har roz nahi) ye bhi puchho:

> Abhi ye na aata to kya reel khol lete? (haan / nahi)

## Agar wo khud "urge" likhe

Turant wahi teen message. `04_log.csv` me `khud_maanga_urge` = Y. Agar aaj ka episode pehle hi ja chuka hai, to pichhle kisi din ka THEEK ya TAAZA add-on bhejo, naya episode nahi.

## Raat (15 minute)

- Har bheje gaye message ki ek line `04_log.csv` me bharo.
- Jo aaj chup raha, use kuch mat bhejo. Agle din bas samay par pack bhejo.

## Bhasha ke niyam

| Karo | Mat karo |
|---|---|
| "Wapas aa gaye, achha laga." | "Kal kyun nahi aaye?" |
| "Aaj ka rest poora." | "Ab padhai karo." |
| "Kal milte hain." | "Phone kam chalao." |
| Galat jawab par: "Kareeb tha. Sahi ye hai, kyunki..." | "Galat." |

Research ke mutabik guilt aaram ko kharab karta hai, aur zabardasti se log bhaag jaate hain.

## Din 7 par (aadha raasta)

Sabko ek chhota message:

> Ek hafta ho gaya. Ek sawal: kahani kaisi lag rahi hai, 1 se 5 me? Aur kya badlun?

**Din 7 ki ginti:** agar 16 me se 5 se kam log active hain (30% se kam), to test rok kar format ya timing badlo. Mujhe log dikhao, hum saath dekhenge.

## Agar koi pareshani wala message bheje

Agar koi likhe ki wo bahut tanav me hai, haar maan raha hai ya khud ko nuksan pahunchane ki baat kare, to pack mat bhejo. Ye bhejo:

> Ye padh kar mujhe chinta hui. Aap akele nahi ho. Tele-MANAS par abhi baat kar sakte ho: 14416 (muft, 24 ghante). Kisi apne ko bhi bata do. Main yahin hoon.

Phir us insaan se khud phone par baat karo. Test usse zyada zaroori nahi hai.

## STOP

Koi "STOP" likhe to turant band karo, "theek hai, shukriya" bhejo, aur uski lines dono CSV se mita do.
