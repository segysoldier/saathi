# Onboarding ke sawal (Din 1-2)

Chat me ek-ek karke puchho, ya inhi sawalon ka Google Form bana lo.
Jawab `04_log.csv` aur `03_people.csv` me bharo.

## 5 sawal

1. **Umar?** (18 se kam ho to vinamrata se mana kar do.)
2. **Abhi kya karte ho?** Student / naukri / tayyari (dropper).
3. **Aapka goal kya hai?** Ek line me. Phir khud tay karo ki wo kis group me aata hai:
   - Medical (NEET, doctor)
   - Tech (engineering, software)
   - Sarkari exam (SSC, PSC, bank)
   - In teeno me na aaye to abhi waiting list me rakho.
4. **Din ke kaun se 2 waqt aap sabse zyada reel/video dekhte ho?** Jaise "bus me subah 8:30" aur "raat 10:30". Ghadi ka time likhwao.
5. **Message kis roop me pasand hai?** Padhna (text) ya sunna (voice note).
6. **Roz kaun sa app kholte ho: WhatsApp, Telegram ya dono?** Test usi par chalao jo wo roz kholte hain. Jawab `03_people.csv` ke note me likho. Ye isliye zaroori hai ki baad me bot Telegram par banana hai, aur hume jaanna hai ki 18-24 ke log Telegram roz kholte bhi hain ya nahi.

## Baseline screenshot

> Ek kaam aur: Settings > Digital Wellbeing > "Dashboard" kholo, "Pichhle 7 din" chuno aur screenshot bhej do. Mujhe sirf Instagram, YouTube aur kul screen time dekhna hai.

Screenshot se ye teen aankde `03_people.csv` me likho:
- Instagram: roz ka ausat minute
- YouTube: roz ka ausat minute
- Kul screen time: roz ka ausat minute

Agar unke phone me Digital Wellbeing nahi hai (kuch purane phone), to Instagram ke andar "Your activity > Time spent" ka screenshot le lo aur note me likh do.

## Ek setup jo zaroor karwana hai

> Hamari chat ko pin kar lo (chat par lamba dabao > Pin). Taaki message dhoondhna na pade.

Research me yahi sabse bada khatra tha: badla agar reel se zyada door hua to log nahi aate.
