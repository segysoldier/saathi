## 3. Paanch interview (10 minute har ek, phone par)

Chuno: 2 jo sabse zyada active the, 2 jo beech me chhod gaye, 1 jo beech ka tha.
Jawab unke shabdon me likho. Beech me samjhao mat.

1. Jab message aata tha, tab aap asal me kya kar rahe hote the?
2. Kis din aapne message nahi khola? Us waqt kya hua tha?
3. Kahani ke baad kaisa lagta tha? Reel ke baad se alag?
4. Kya kabhi kahani ke baad bhi reel kholi? Kitni der?
5. Thaka / theek / taaza me se sabse zyada kya chuna, aur kyun?
6. Kya pehle aapko pata tha ki aapka time itna kahan ja raha tha?
7. Sabse bekaar cheez kya thi?
8. Agar ye kal band ho jaye to kya aap ise miss karoge? Kyun ya kyun nahi?
