# Bharti message (16 log chahiye: 8 students, 8 naukri wale, umar 18-24)

Ye message WhatsApp/Telegram group, Instagram story ya seedhe doston ko bhejo.
Wada sirf wahi karo jo likha hai.

## Chhota version (group ya story ke liye)

> Ek chhota 14 din ka experiment kar raha hoon. 16 log chahiye, umar 18-24.
>
> Idea simple hai: jab aap thak kar phone uthate ho, us waqt main aapko 5 minute ki ek chhoti kahani bhejunga jo aapke apne goal ki duniya ki hogi (doctor / tech / sarkari naukri). Kahani khatam hoti hai, reel ki tarah chalti nahi rehti.
>
> Aapko kya karna hai: din me 1-2 message kholna aur ek-do button jaisa jawab dena. Bas.
> Paisa nahi lagega. Koi app install nahi karni.
>
> Interested ho to "Saathi" likh kar reply karo.

## Lamba version (jo "Saathi" likhe, use ye bhejo)

> Thanks! Ye raha poora hisaab:
>
> **Kya hoga**
> - 14 din. Pehle 2 din setup, phir 10 din roz 1-2 message, aakhri 2 din feedback.
> - Har message me aapke goal ki duniya ki ek chhoti kahani hogi (2-3 minute). Mann ho to ek mazedaar sawal ya 2 minute ka chhota kaam bhi.
> - Aap batayenge ki din ke kaun se 2 waqt aap sabse zyada scroll karte ho. Main usi waqt bhejunga.
>
> **Mujhe aapse kya chahiye**
> 1. Aapka goal aur 2 "khatarnak waqt".
> 2. Shuru me aur ant me phone ke Digital Wellbeing ka ek screenshot (sirf app ka time dikhna chahiye, aur kuch nahi).
> 3. Har message ke baad ek-do shabd ka jawab.
>
> **Aapka data**
> - Sirf main dekhunga. Kisi ko nahi dunga, kahin post nahi karunga.
> - Aap kabhi bhi "STOP" likh kar nikal sakte ho, aur main aapka data mita dunga.
> - Ye padhai ya ilaaj ki salaah nahi hai. Kahaniyan aur sawal AI ki madad se bane hain aur maine khud check kiye hain.
>
> **Shart:** umar 18 ya usse zyada.
>
> Theek lage to "Haan, shuru karo" likho, aur main 5 chhote sawal bhejunga.

## Dhyaan rakho

- Jo "Haan, shuru karo" na likhe, use test me mat jodo. Yahi unki sehmati hai; iska screenshot rakh lo.
- 18 se kam umar wale ko mat jodo.
- 16 se zyada log aa jayein to waiting list bana lo. Zyada log ek saath sambhalna mushkil hoga.
